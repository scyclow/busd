How To Burn

Participants who submit a valid US banknote, accompanied with a completed Burn Application, shall receive a proportional amount of bUSD and a Proof of Burn NFT.

To submit USD for an official Burn Ceremony, please complete the following steps:

  1. Read the Burn Agreement in full.
  2. Sign and complete a Burn Application.

    OR

    Mint a BurnAgreement NFT and complete a Simplified Burn Application.
  3. Mail an envelope including:
    • A completed Burn Application
    • All bills you wish to burn
    • All required processing fees ($50 or 1 BurnAgreement NFT per submitted bill)
    to the following address:
PLEASE CONTACT THE BURN AGENT FOR MAILING INSTRUCTIONS

Please double check that all information is correct before mailing.

All bills received by the Burn Agent shall be burned during an official Burn Ceremony subject to the terms of the Burn Agreement.

Reference the Burn Agreement for all policies regarding bill return, bill eligibility, bill custody, and bill forfeit.

If you mail cash over $500, USPS requires you to use Registered Mail® for proper insurance and protection. The Burn Agent shall not be responsible for lost, stolen, or improperly sent mail.

How Does It Work?

  1. Participants submit valid US currency to the Burn Agent.
  2. The Burn Agent performs a Burn Ceremony, in which the submitted bill is lit on fire.
  3. The Burn Agent issues bUSD to the participant at a one-to-one rate* with the denomination of the submitted bill once it has been completely burned.
  4. The Proof of Burn is published.

Burn Agreement

Read The Burn Agreement

The bUSD Burn Agreement outlines all legal matters pertaining to the bUSD project, including valuation, legality, the Burn Ceremony, Proof of Burn media, bUSD reciept, the Burn Agent, issuance authorization, bUSD supply, bill custody, bill forfeit, return policy, bill eligibility, burn scheduling, processing fees, privacy policy, intellectual property, and warranty.

Mint an Agreement NFT

Burn Agreement NFTs, which display the bUSD Burn Agreement in full, may be used in place of a processing fee as part of a simplified bUSD application process. To use an Agreement NFT, please follow the standard Burn Application process using a completed Simplified Burn Application instead of the standard Application in Step 2. Each Agreement NFT may be used to exempt an individual bill's processing fee.
